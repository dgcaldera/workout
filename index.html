Coach's Rules For You

No 1-rep-max testing, ever, at your age — strength is built through clean reps at moderate-to-hard effort. Every new movement gets a light warm-up set first. Weight jumps are small on purpose (2.5–5 lb / 1–2.5 kg at a time). If a lift below is flagged for a spotter or rack safety, don't load it heavy alone. Sleep and food matter more than any set/rep scheme at your age — the program only works if you recover.
