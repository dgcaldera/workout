Coach's Rules For You

No 1-rep-max testing, ever, at your age — strength is built through clean reps at moderate-to-hard effort. Every new or heavier movement gets a light warm-up set first. Barbell lifts stay conservative: small jumps (2.5–5 lb / 1–2.5 kg), rack safety pins or a spotter on squat and bench, and you only move up when every rep of every set is clean. Sleep and food matter more than any set/rep scheme — the program only works if you recover.
